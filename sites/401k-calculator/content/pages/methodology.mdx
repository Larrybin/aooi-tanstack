# 401k Calculator Methodology

This page documents the calculation order and scope used by the 401k Calculator. The implementation and explanation were last technically reviewed on July 31, 2026.

## Annual Projection Order

The calculator projects one year at a time. In each projected year it:

1. increases salary by the selected annual salary increase;
2. calculates the employee contribution from that updated salary;
3. limits the matchable contribution percentage to the lower of the employee contribution percentage and match limit;
4. calculates employer match from the updated salary, eligible match percentage, and employer match percentage; and
5. applies the annual return to the prior balance before adding that year's employee contribution and employer match.

The projected monthly retirement income is an illustration equal to 4% of the final projected balance divided by 12.

## What Is Not Modeled

The estimate does not model taxes, investment fees, IRS contribution limits, catch-up contributions, vesting schedules, plan loans, withdrawals, required minimum distributions, inflation-adjusted spending, or changing market returns. It assumes the entered percentages remain constant throughout the projection.

## Local Processing

Calculator inputs and results are processed in the browser. No account or database is required for the calculation.

## Appropriate Use

Use the output to compare hypothetical scenarios, not as a guarantee or as financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.
