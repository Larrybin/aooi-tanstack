# Privacy Policy

401k Calculator performs projection calculations locally in your browser.

## Calculator Inputs

The values you enter are used to update the estimate in the current browser session. The calculator does not require an account and does not submit calculator inputs to a backend service.

## Local Processing

Projection results are calculated on your device. Do not enter personal identifiers or other sensitive information into the calculator.

## Contact

Questions can be sent to support@401k-calculator.net.
