# Privacy Policy

Random Group Generator creates group assignments in your current browser page without requiring an account.

## Names and Rosters

Names entered into the generator are held in the page while you use the tool. The generator does not send the roster to a backend service.

## Exports

Copy, CSV download, and print actions are initiated by your browser. Downloaded or copied results are then controlled by your device and the applications where you share them.

## Sensitive Information

Names may be personal information. Review your browser, extensions, shared-screen setup, and local privacy requirements before entering or displaying a roster.

## Contact

Questions can be sent to support@randomgroupgenerator.org.
