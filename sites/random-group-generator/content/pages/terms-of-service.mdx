# Terms of Service

Random Group Generator is provided as a browser-based utility for creating randomized group assignments.

## Acceptable Use

Use the tool only with information you are permitted to handle. Do not use it for unlawful, harmful, deceptive, or discriminatory activity.

## Review Required

Generated groups are a randomized first draft, not a guarantee of fairness, safety, compatibility, or balanced skills. Review and adjust assignments when attendance, accessibility, conflicts, safeguarding, or other real-world constraints matter.

## Availability

The service is provided as available and may change or be interrupted. Keep a separate copy of any group assignment you need to retain.

## Contact

Questions can be sent to support@randomgroupgenerator.org.
