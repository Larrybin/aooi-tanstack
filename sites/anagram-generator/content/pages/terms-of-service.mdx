# Terms of Service

Anagram Generator is provided as a browser-based utility for exploring possible word and phrase combinations.

## Acceptable Use

Use the tool only for lawful purposes. Do not use it for harmful, deceptive, abusive, or rights-infringing activity.

## Review Required

Results are suggestions from a limited word list and preset phrases. They are not an official dictionary ruling, a guarantee that every letter is used in every single-word suggestion, or confirmation that a result is accepted by a particular game, puzzle, classroom, or publication.

## Availability

The service is provided as available and may change or be interrupted. Keep a separate copy of results you need to retain.
