# Privacy Policy

Anagram Generator creates word and phrase suggestions in your current browser page without requiring an account.

## Text You Enter

Letters, words, phrases, and filters entered into the generator are held in the page while you use the tool. The generator does not send that working text to a backend service.

## Copy and Downloads

Clipboard and TXT download actions are initiated by your browser. Copied or downloaded results are then controlled by your device and the applications where you save or share them.

## Sensitive Information

Do not enter confidential, private, or regulated information. Browser extensions, shared screens, device software, and other services outside this generator may have their own data practices.

## Contact

Questions can be sent to support@anagram-generator.net.
